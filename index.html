How to read the experience

The game is the assumption.
The cues are the investigation.

01
Evidence in our record

Navigation can combine inputs.

Landmarks, route memory, smell, the sun, magnetic information, and sometimes flockmates may contribute.

02
Invented for interaction

You collect cues by flying through rings.

Real pigeons do not collect floating symbols. The rings, meter, order, and skyline turn a complex process into a playable system.

03
Uncertain · left out

Not every proposed sense is active.

Infrared vision, polarized light, and infrasound are not game cues because our notes mark their evidence as limited or mixed.
